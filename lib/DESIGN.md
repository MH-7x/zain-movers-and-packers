## Brand & Style

This design system projects the authoritative reliability of an established enterprise paired with the meticulous care of white-glove relocation services in the UAE. Moving is inherently chaotic and anxiety-inducing; the digital presence counters this by presenting calm, structural certitude, architectural grid layouts, and high-end editorial clarity.

### Personality & Tone
- **Authoritative & Established:** Operating with pride since 2015 across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and the wider UAE. Visual pacing reflects institutional permanence rather than startup transience.
- **Meticulous & Clear:** Logistical precision communicated through deliberate typographic scales, visible baseline rules, clean numbered sequences (`01`, `02`, `03`), and sharp data density.
- **Warm & Reassuring:** A palette grounded in deep mineral charcoal, crisp linen whites, and a focused terracotta accent delivers warmth without sacrificing corporate credibility.

### Design Movements & Execution
The system rejects ephemeral SaaS tropes—no amorphous gradient blobs, no floating cards with heavy diffuse dropshadows, no nested pill buttons, and no symmetrical three-column card templates. Instead, it draws from **high-end architectural editorial design** and **Swiss typographic discipline**:
- **Asymmetric Composition:** Off-center 55/45 splits, wide-margin text blocks, and structured list layouts that direct the eye down an unbroken narrative flow.
- **Cartographic & Logistical Precision:** Crisp hairline dividers, monoline boundary definitions, structured tabular rows, and bold index numbering.
- **Conversion-Critical Accessibility:** Immediate visual paths to communication channels—most notably direct phone access (`055 4495331`) and dedicated verification markers.

## Colors

The color system utilizes high-contrast structural darks against warm, tactile surfaces, deploying the vibrant terracotta accent with surgical restraint to denote action and directional focus.

### Primary Palette Roles
- **Primary Action (Terracotta / Warm Coral):** `#D95338` | `oklch(0.6415 0.2194 30.8571)`. Reserved strictly for primary conversion CTAs, active pagination steps, critical notification badges, and active tab rules. Never used as large background fills.
- **Foreground & Structure (Deep Charcoal):** `#1F1F1F` | `oklch(0.2686 0 0)`. The primary typographic ink for headings (H1–H3), high-contrast utility bars, dark trust badges, footers, and crisp hairline dividers.
- **Secondary Surface (Editorial Cool Gray):** `#F5F6F7` | `oklch(0.9670 0.0029 264.5419)`. Applied to full-width alternating editorial section bands, structured data tables, and input field backgrounds.
- **Tertiary Accent Surface (Warm Linen Tint):** `#FDF8F3` | `oklch(0.9869 0.0214 95.2774)`. Deployed for trust badges, quotation banners, VIP relocation callouts, and guarantee panels.
- **Base Surface (Pure White):** `#FFFFFF` | `oklch(1 0 0)`. The primary canvas for maximum contrast and legibility.

### Specialized Accents & Rules
- **Direct Messaging Green:** `#25D366`. Strictly quarantined for authentic WhatsApp actions, icons, and status indicators. Never repurposed for generic success alerts or unrelated iconography.
- **Hairline Borders:** `rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.12)` on light surfaces; `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` on dark charcoal surfaces.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes the literary, timeless gravity of `Source Serif 4` with the neutral, engineering efficiency of `Inter`.

### Heading Typographic Rules
- Use `Source Serif 4` strictly for editorial headlines, section titles, pull quotes, and oversized section index markers (`01`, `02`).
- Headings require deliberate linebreaks to preserve balanced rag and dramatic asymmetry. Never allow single-word orphan lines in display sizes.
- `label-eyebrow` must always be rendered in full uppercase with a tracking of `0.12em` to frame major headlines.

### Functional UI & Body Rules
- Use `Inter` for all descriptive text, moving schedules, pricing estimates, specifications, form fields, and navigation links.
- Numbers within data sheets and quote calculators must use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve optical column alignments.
- Primary phone representations (`055 4495331`) rendered in headings adopt `Source Serif 4 SemiBold`; in utility bars and interactive UI, they adopt `Inter SemiBold` with slight positive tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts eschew floating isolated blocks in favor of architectural, edge-anchored structural bands based on a 12-column grid.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with 32px (`2rem`) gutters and minimum 64px (`4rem`) outer margins. Content max-width is pinned to 1320px.
- **Asymmetric Splits:** The default hero and major service sections deploy an off-center 7:5 (58% / 42%) or 8:4 column split. Text narratives claim the larger column; quote engines, visual proof, or data manifests occupy the counterweight column.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 24px gutters, 32px margins. Splits collapse gracefully into 4:4 pairs or stacked bands.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px margins. Numbered lists and asymmetric pairs reflow to a strict single-column top-to-bottom sequence.

### Structural Rhythms
- Sections are delineated by full-width tone switches (`#FFFFFF` to `#F5F6F7`) or single horizontal rules (`1px solid rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.12)`).
- Vertical pacing between major thematic shifts uses `space-3xl` (96px). Inner group spacing relies systematically on `space-md` (16px) and `space-xl` (40px).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately avoids diffuse drop shadows, multi-layered floating elevations, and neo-skeuomorphism. Depth is communicated strictly through surface layering, structural borders, and flat architectural containment.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Secondary Linen Surface (`#F5F6F7`).
- **Level 1 (Structural Inset / Framing):** Defined not by shadow, but by crisp `1px` borders in `rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.12)` or contrasting surface fills (e.g., `#FDF8F3`).
- **Level 2 (Active Dropdowns & Popovers):** High-contrast structural overlay using `#FFFFFF` with a crisp `1px` border in `#1F1F1F`, supported by a direct, controlled shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Dark Foundation Bands):** Full-bleed `#1F1F1F` blocks featuring pure white text and subtle hairline borders in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` used to anchor corporate trust and service credentials.

## Shapes

The shape system employs pure architectural geometry: **Sharp (`0px` roundedness)** throughout. 

### Implementation Directives
- **Zero Radii Everywhere:** All buttons, input fields, notification badges, image containers, modal windows, and structural dividers have `border-radius: 0px`. 
- **Industrial Metaphor:** This crisp, unrounded treatment echoes the physical geometry of shipping containers, crates, architectural blueprints, and high-end editorial layouts. It deliberately rejects the playful, rounded appearance common to consumer tech, instilling formal trust and commercial rigor.
- **Accents:** Active step indicators and list item bullets are styled as sharp micro-squares (4px by 4px) rather than soft circles.

## Components

### 1. Header & Utility Bar
- **Top Utility Rail:** Deep Charcoal background (`#1F1F1F`), 40px height. Contains operating years ("Serving the UAE Since 2015"), operating license credentials, and primary direct phone (`055 4495331`) linked with an instant call trigger.
- **Main Nav Bar:** Pure White, 80px height, bottom hairline border (`1px solid rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.1)`). Asymmetric layout: Brand wordmark left-aligned; editorial links center-aligned; secondary WhatsApp trigger and primary "Request Quote" CTA right-aligned.

### 2. Primary & Secondary Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Terracotta background (`#D95338`), pure white typography, `border-radius: 0px`, zero border, vertical padding 16px, horizontal padding 32px. Hover state: subtle darken (`oklch(0.58 0.22 30.85)`). Transition: 150ms ease-in-out.
- **Secondary CTA:** Deep Charcoal background (`#1F1F1F`), pure white typography, `0px` radius. Hover state: Terracotta (`#D95338`).
- **Outline / Editorial Link:** Transparent background, `1px solid #1F1F1F`, dark text. Hover state: `#1F1F1F` background with white text.
- **WhatsApp Direct Action:** `#25D366` background, pure white icon and text, strictly deployed with the official WhatsApp glyph for instant moving estimates.

### 3. Numbered Editorial Lists (Process / Services)
- Avoid 3-card grid containers. Instead, present workflows as a continuous horizontal or vertical list divided by 1px rules.
- **Index Marker:** Oversized `Source Serif 4` numeral (`01`, `02`, `03`) in `#1F1F1F` or muted `#D95338`.
- **Content Alignment:** Two-column grid per list item: Left column houses the index and service title; right column houses the technical scope and move logistics.

### 4. Form Fields & Quote Request Engine
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#F5F6F7`, `0px` border-radius, bottom border `2px solid #1F1F1F`, top/left/right border `none`. Height 52px. Internal padding 16px. On focus: bottom border shifts to Terracotta (`#D95338`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square indicators (18px by 18px), `1px solid #1F1F1F`. Active state: filled with Deep Charcoal (`#1F1F1F`) featuring a crisp internal square mark.

### 5. Proof & Metrics Strips
- Structural full-width rows divided by vertical hairline rules. 
- Metric values rendered in `Source Serif 4` display scale with small, uppercase `Inter` metric labels beneath (e.g., "12,000+ RELOCATIONS COMPLETED", "99.4% ON-TIME DISPATCH").
